The Problem

Video storage is quietly eating your budget

Camera counts keep climbing. Resolution keeps rising. HDD prices keep spiking. Throwing more disks and bandwidth at the problem is expensive, slow, and unsustainable.

Hard-drive price surges

Retention requirements keep growing while disk costs climb — forcing painful trade-offs between retention depth and budget.

Lowering bitrate destroys evidence value

Dialing down quality to save space degrades forensic detail and breaks downstream AI analytics — a non-starter for security teams.

Narrow-bandwidth bottlenecks

Remote sites, mobile units and satellite links can't move 4K/8K video reliably — limiting real-time command and response.

The Minova Difference

Compression that starts in the chip, not the software

Titan V uses a dedicated RISC-V intelligent video processor to compress video streams in real time at the silicon level — no re-encoding, no transcoding lag, no quality sacrifice.

STEP 01

Ingest any standard stream

Native support for mainstream protocols and codecs — plug Titan V in-line with your existing cameras, NVR/VMS and storage with zero architecture change.

STEP 02

Chip-level intelligent encoding

The RISC-V core applies compressed-sensing and deep-learning algorithms to shrink video 3–20× in real time, preserving every structural parameter.

STEP 03

Store more, transmit further

Compressed streams either write directly to local storage (Storage Appliance) or stream back over narrow links (Transmission Appliance) — with AI analysis untouched.
